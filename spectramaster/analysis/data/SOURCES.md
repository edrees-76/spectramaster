## Rebuilding

    python spectramaster/analysis/data/build_coincidences_ensdf.py path/to/livechart/raw
    # check: SPECTRAMASTER_LIVECHART_DIR=path/to/livechart/raw pytest tests/test_coincidence.py
    # independent Monte Carlo check: python -m spectramaster.validation.coincidence_ensdf_check

`path/to/livechart/raw` holds `decay_rads_g/` and `decay_rads_bp/` with one `<A><el>.csv` per
nuclide (e.g. `152eu.csv`). Method (docs/baseline/ensdf_coincidence_protocol.md):

- Each gamma is placed by its start and end levels; a level branches in proportion to the
  transition intensities I (1 + alpha); a level living longer than 1 us ends the cascade.
- P(j | i) = probability that the path from the end level of i passes through j, divided by
  (1 + alpha_j); it does not depend on how the levels are fed.
- P(beta+ | i) from the beta+ feeding of each level (`decay_rads_bp`), stored as ``ann``.
- Gammas of the same tabulated energy from different levels are one line.
- An included daughter whose gammas the parent's ENSDF decay already lists (Pb-207m of Bi-207,
  Ba-137m of Cs-137 ...) is in ``daughters_listed_by_parent``; its IT record is not added again.

Not described: X-ray - gamma summing (EC nuclides such as Ba-133, Eu-152), angular correlation.
Assumptions: every positron annihilates in the source and the two photons are independent at
first order.

The previous builder, `build_coincidences.py` (SandiaDecay `sandia.decay.min.xml`, 511 keV
summing reconstructed from the beta+ branch endpoints, docs/baseline/annihilation_summing_protocol.md),
is kept for reference.

The gamma library:

    python spectramaster/analysis/data/build_library.py            # uses the cached IAEA extract
    python spectramaster/analysis/data/build_library.py --ndlab path/to/ndlab_db.s3db -v

## Library conventions

* Entry intensity = photons per **100 decays of the entry nuclide**, including the short-lived
  daughters listed in `includes` (auto-included when T½ < 10 min and not an entry of their own, or
  listed explicitly), weighted by branching × equilibrium activity ratio λd/(λd−λp)
  (secular or transient equilibrium: Cs-137→Ba-137m 0.947, Ba-140→La-140 1.152, Te-132→I-132,
  Ce-144→Pr-144, Ru-106→Rh-106, Mo-99→Tc-99m, ...).
* Natural-series members (Th-234, Pa-234m, Ra-226, Pb-214, Bi-214, Pb-210, U-235 chain, Ac-228,
  Th-228, Ra-224, Pb-212, Bi-212, Tl-208 ...) are separate entries with their own per-decay
  intensities; `series` names the chain, `series_factor` is the member's activity relative to the
  series head in secular equilibrium (Tl-208 0.3594, Pa-234 0.0016), `subgroup` the usual
  sub-chain label (Ra-226, Th-228, Ra-228 daughters).
* Line kinds: `g` gamma, `x` X-ray (K lines ≥ 1 %, merged per K-alpha1/alpha2/beta group; L lines
  only from IAEA), `a` 511 keV annihilation (2 × positron branch).
* Gammas kept: 10–3600 keV, ≥ 0.05 % (weak emitters such as Pu-239: ≥ 2 % of their strongest
  line), at most 40 strongest lines per nuclide.
* Values: IAEA ENSDF (`src` = "I") whenever the line exists there, SandiaDecay otherwise ("S").
  `provenance.flags` lists every line ≥ 1 % where the two sources differ by > 0.1 keV or > 5 %
  relative intensity, or where a line exists in only one source (229 flags since 2026-10-21, e.g.
  Th-234 63.3 keV: IAEA 3.7 % vs Sandia 4.85 %; Lu-177m, Ho-166m, W-187, Tb-160).

## Emission-probability uncertainties (added 2026-10-15)

`gamma_library.json` lines from IAEA ENSDF (`src` "I") carry a 5th value: the standard uncertainty
of the intensity (photons per 100 decays) from the same ndlab extract (`intensity_unc` column),
combined in quadrature over merged entries and scaled like the intensity (2542 of 2732 lines;
none for SandiaDecay-only lines or where ENSDF states none). Energies and intensities are unchanged
(`build_library.py` asserts that the uncertainty-carrying merge reproduces them).

Independent cross-check: LNHB/DDEP recommended data (Laboratoire National Henri Becquerel, LARA
files `https://www.lnhb.fr/nuclides/<nuclide>.lara.txt`, downloaded 2026-10-15 for 126 of the 200
library nuclides; kept outside the repository in `D:/SpectraMaster_public_data/lnhb_lara`, not
bundled). `python -m spectramaster.validation.ddep_check`: 364 single gamma lines >= 1 % of 81
nuclides, 94.8 % agree within 2 combined standard uncertainties and 97.3 % within 3 (363 lines and 97.2 % before 2026-10-21). Largest
differences: Cu-67 (DDEP 2026 re-evaluation, about 10 %), Ra-223 (about 10 %), Nd-147, Ho-166m,
Np-239; Mn-54/Co-60/Sc-46 appear only because the library rounds intensities to 4 significant
digits. The IAEA LiveChart API was not used: it answers automated requests with a browser
challenge.

## Resolved K-beta X-rays of heavy elements (added 2026-10-15)

ENSDF gives one K-beta X-ray line per element at a weighted energy (Th 106.89 keV) that lies between
the two peaks a germanium detector resolves. `build_library.py` (`KBETA`, `split_kbeta`) splits it,
for W ... Cm, into K'beta1 and K'beta2 with the energies and the K'beta1 share (0.74-0.79) of the
LNHB/DDEP recommended data (same intensity in total). Only X-ray lines of 20 nuclides change; no
gamma line changes (the line cap does not count the added K'beta2 line).
Effect (measured before/after): Eu-155 reported in 12 -> 2 of the 20 longest laboratory spectra and
no longer among the frequent implausible IAEA IDB identifications (53 -> below the top 15); IDB
HPGe implausible 8.82 -> 8.15 per spectrum; U-235 / Pu-239 recall and all synthetic criteria
unchanged; informational NaI false identifications with the environmental library 0.29 -> 0.375.

## Wrong, duplicated and lost lines (added 2026-10-21)

Found by the certified-reference-material check (`docs/baseline/crm_validation_protocol.md`, D1).
Protocol and results: `docs/baseline/library_lines_protocol.md`; line-by-line diff:
`docs/baseline/after_LB1/library_diff.txt`. `build_library.py` changes:

* **Lost IAEA lines.** A daughter's IAEA line was compared with every line added before it,
  including the daughter's own, and dropped when within 0.5 keV of one. It is now compared only with
  the members before it. Eight lines are restored from IAEA, for example Np-239 228.183 keV (Am-243)
  and I-132 727.0 / 621.2 keV (Te-132). Before, most of them held the Sandia value.
* **Duplicated lines** (`DUP_TOL_KEV`, `DUP_RATIO`). A Sandia line not paired with an IAEA line is
  dropped when an IAEA line of the entry, gamma or X-ray, lies within 1.0 keV with 0.5-2 times its
  intensity. This removes 42 lines, for example:
  * Hf-181 482.0 keV (99.3 %, listed again under Ta-181m; the library held about 180 % at 482 keV);
  * Np-237 94.66 / 98.44 / 111.3 keV (X-rays listed as gammas);
  * Ta-182 69.34 keV;
  * Sb-124 1325.0 keV;
  * Pa-234 1393.57 keV;
  * Sb-126 278.6 / 989.3 keV.

  Known misclassification: Pa-234 824.2 keV is a separate line (DDEP lists 824.2 and 825.1 keV). It
  was outside the 40-line cap before and after, so the library content does not change.
* **Correction table** (`CORRECTIONS`). It holds IAEA values contradicted by both SandiaDecay and
  DDEP. Only Ac-228 674.75 keV is in it: 2.1 % becomes 0.105 % +- 0.010 % (DDEP), which the measured
  RGTh-1 spectra confirm. At 0.105 % the line falls below the 40-line cap, so it is no longer in the
  library. The record in `provenance.corrections` stays.
* `ddep_check` also lists gross ratio differences against the nearest DDEP gamma within 0.5 keV, and
  lines with no DDEP line within 0.5 keV. That second list is the blind spot of the 0.1 keV match.
  Remaining entries: Yb-169 63.01 keV (IAEA and Sandia, not in DDEP) and four Sb-127 lines (IAEA
  energies 0.6-1.1 keV above DDEP). Both are unresolved and unchanged. The gross list skips a line
  when |z| <= 3, so it would not have caught the Ac-228 line, whose ENSDF uncertainty is 0.7 %.
