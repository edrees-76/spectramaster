The LiveChart REST API (`https://www-nds.iaea.org/relnsd/v1/data?fields=decay_rads...`) answers
automated requests with a Cloudflare browser challenge, so the same IAEA data were taken from the
IAEA's own published ndlab database instead (40 MB zip, kept outside the repository; only the
extract is bundled).

## Rebuilding

    python spectramaster/analysis/data/build_library.py            # uses the cached IAEA extract
    python spectramaster/analysis/data/build_library.py --ndlab path/to/ndlab_db.s3db -v

## Library conventions

* Entry intensity = photons per **100 decays of the entry nuclide**, including the short-lived
  daughters listed in `includes` (auto-included when T½ < 10 min and not an entry of their own, or
  listed explicitly), weighted by branching × equilibrium activity ratio λd/(λd−λp)
  (secular or transient equilibrium: Cs-137→Ba-137m 0.947, Ba-140→La-140 1.152, Te-132→I-132,
  Ce-144→Pr-144, Ru-106→Rh-106, Mo-99→Tc-99m, ...).
* Natural-series members (Th-234, Pa-234m, Ra-226, Pb-214, Bi-214, Pb-210, U-235 chain, Ac-228,
  Th-228, Ra-224, Pb-212, Bi-212, Tl-208 ...) are separate entries with their own per-decay
  intensities; `series` names the chain, `series_factor` is the member's activity relative to the
  series head in secular equilibrium (Tl-208 0.3594, Pa-234 0.0016), `subgroup` the usual
  sub-chain label (Ra-226, Th-228, Ra-228 daughters).
* Line kinds: `g` gamma, `x` X-ray (K lines ≥ 1 %, merged per K-alpha1/alpha2/beta group; L lines
  only from IAEA), `a` 511 keV annihilation (2 × positron branch).
* Gammas kept: 10–3600 keV, ≥ 0.05 % (weak emitters such as Pu-239: ≥ 2 % of their strongest
  line), at most 40 strongest lines per nuclide.
* Values: IAEA ENSDF (`src` = "I") whenever the line exists there, SandiaDecay otherwise ("S").
  `provenance.flags` lists every line ≥ 1 % where the two sources differ by > 0.1 keV or > 5 %
  relative intensity, or where a line exists in only one source (232 flags in 66 nuclides, e.g.
  Th-234 63.3 keV: IAEA 3.7 % vs Sandia 4.85 %; Lu-177m, Ho-166m, W-187, Tb-160).
